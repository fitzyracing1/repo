# RR-01 propellant load — 20% methane

Locked pad allocation. Mass only. Not a mix recipe, not a chamber procedure.

## Ratio

4 kg liquid oxygen : 1 kg liquid methane.

That is the stoichiometric mass ratio for \(\mathrm{CH_4 + 2\,O_2}\).
Methane fraction \(= 1 / (1 + 4) = 0.20\).

Total usable propellant on the pad: **18,000 kg**.

| | Mass | Share | Volume (liquid) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Methane | **3,600 kg** | 20% | 8.53 m³ |
| Liquid oxygen | **14,400 kg** | 80% | 12.62 m³ |
| Total | 18,000 kg | 100% | — |

Densities used for volume only: LCH4 422 kg/m³, LOX 1,141 kg/m³.

## This flight (soft landing)

Same 18 t load. Burn does not change the *ratio*; it burns both tanks down together.

| | Methane | LOX | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loaded | 3,600 kg | 14,400 kg | 18,000 kg |
| Burned | 1,399 kg | 5,596 kg | 6,995 kg |
| Left at touchdown | 2,201 kg | 8,804 kg | 11,005 kg |

## What this is not

- Not 3.5:1 (that was the earlier fuel-rich paper allocation, 22% methane).
- Not 4:1 by volume.
- Not a license to formulate, load, or fire anything.

The flight model still uses a single lumped propellant mass. The 20% split is bookkeeping on that mass.
